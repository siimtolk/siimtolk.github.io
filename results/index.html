All speed numbers are TPS (tokens per second); higher is better. Under each setup name: quantization · uncensoring method · author of the weights, then how it was served (stock = not uncensored; undisclosed = the author does not say how). Click a column to sort; click a setup name to open its weights on Hugging Face; the grey line under it says how it was served. Graded = passed/runs without Estonian and uncensored; uncensored = edgy but harmless tasks answered instead of refused (counted separately, not in graded); thinking off = the same suite with reasoning disabled; follow-up = mean time to first token of the 2nd and later turns in two alternating 13k-token chats; 2 at once = total TPS with two simultaneous requests. The test-area columns (tool use, agent tasks, reasoning, extraction, code, hard tasks with long context and prompt injection, following instructions, judging) show passed/runs for each area; together they make up the graded total. A dash means that test was not run for that setup.
